Part 3 of *Ascendance of a Bookworm*, the *Adopted Daughter of an Archduke* arc, is where the series stopped being a cosy book-making story and became a full-blooded political drama with our favourite book gremlin at its centre. We've argued about this list for days, and we're confident in it. *So let's jump in.*

---

## 5. The Winter Devouring Claim

The opening stretch of Part 3 wastes no time reminding us how dangerous this world really is. The Devouring that gives *Rozemyne* her mana is a death sentence for commoners, and watching that reality close in on her again after the relative safety of Part 2 was a gut punch. **It set the stakes for everything that follows: without a noble family, Myne dies.** Simple as that.

---

## 4. Delia's Fate and the Temple's Rot

Part 3 doesn't shy away from showing how ugly the temple's power structures are, and *Delia's* storyline is the sharpest example. Used as a pawn by the corrupt *High Bishop Bezewanst* from the moment she could walk, her arc punctures any illusion that the temple is a safe little sanctuary. **Her ending is one of the most quietly devastating payoffs in the whole series**, and it cemented Part 3 as the arc where *Bookworm* earned its dramatic chops.

---

## 3. Karstedt's Family Embraces Myne

Before the grand adoption could happen, *Myne* had to be folded into a knight's family first, and watching the *Karstedt* household take her in was genuinely heartwarming. *Elvira* and *Cornelius* going from wary strangers to genuine protectors gave the political machinery a human face. **It's the moment the cast around Myne started feeling like a real family rather than a network of allies.**

---

## 2. The Battle in the Temple

*Bezewanst's* desperate power play, using *Bindewald* and a small army to seize *Myne* by force, gave us the arc's explosive climax. Watching *Karstedt* and *Ferdinand* cut down knights while *Myne* defended herself with raw, uncontrolled mana was thrilling. **It was the first time the series showed us what noble warfare actually looks like, and it knocked our socks off.**

---

## 1. Rozemyne's Adoption and the Jureve Slumber

Was it ever going to be anything else? *Myne* formally becoming *Rozemyne*, adopted daughter of the archduke, is the single most defining moment of Part 3, and it's made bittersweet by the price: **a two-year Jureve sleep to compress her rampaging mana, right as she finally gets the status to make books at scale.** The girl who just wanted to read has to lose two years of her life to survive her own power. That combination of triumph and sacrifice is *Ascendance of a Bookworm* at its absolute best, and nothing else in the arc comes close.

---

Part 3 took *Bookworm* from charming slow-life fantasy to something far more ambitious, and these five moments are why we still rank it among the best arcs in modern isekai. *Rozemyne* might sleep through the consequences, but we never will.
