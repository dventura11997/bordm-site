We're just going to say it: **Beerus is still more powerful than Goku, and it isn't particularly close.** The power scaling discourse in the *Dragon Ball Super* fandom has convinced itself that Goku's Ultra Instinct closes the gap. It doesn't. Every piece of evidence from the show points the other way, and we're going to walk through it.

## Beerus Has Never Actually Tried

The single most important fact in this debate: **Beerus has never fought Goku at full power.** Not once. In their first battle during *Battle of Gods*, Beerus used a fraction of his strength against Super Saiyan God Goku. When Goku pushed him further, Beerus acknowledged the Saiyan's promise, but the god was never in genuine danger.

Even in the *Dragon Ball Super* series proper, Beerus treats Earth's threats as annoyances rather than challenges. He flicked aside attacks that would have levelled Goku and Vegeta in early arcs. A fighter who has never needed to go all out is, by definition, a fighter whose ceiling we haven't seen. **You cannot surpass someone whose limits have never been tested.**

---

## Ultra Instinct Isn't the Equaliser Fans Think

Yes, Goku achieving *Ultra Instinct* against *Jiren* in the Tournament of Power was magnificent. We cheered along with everyone else. But here's the catch: **Ultra Instinct is a technique, not a power level.** It grants Goku godly reflexes and autonomous movement, yet the form drained him so badly in the tournament that he collapsed after using it.

Whis, who has watched Beerus train for millennia, still regards Beerus as a work in progress, but one whose raw destructive capacity dwarfs the mortals below him. Goku mastering a technique that angels use casually doesn't put him on the tier of a God of Destruction. It puts him on the first rung of a very long ladder.

> 🤯 Beerus remains the only character in *Dragon Ball Super* whose true power has never been shown on screen.

---

## Hakai Is a Win Condition Goku Cannot Answer

Forget raw strength for a second and look at abilities. Beerus wields **Hakai**, the power of destruction, which erases matter from existence. We've seen Goku attempt to learn destruction techniques in the manga, and his version is clumsy and incomplete by comparison.

In a genuine fight to the finish, Beerus doesn't need to outpunch Goku. He needs one clean moment to erase him. **Goku has no defence against a serious Hakai from a trained God of Destruction.** No transformation in the series has ever been shown to survive it.

---

## The Verdict

We understand the temptation to crown Goku. He's our hero, he keeps evolving, and Ultra Instinct looks incredible. But wanting something to be true doesn't make it so. Beerus holds back in every encounter, wields an erasure technique Goku can't counter, and has never once revealed his full strength.

**Until Goku fights Beerus seriously and wins on screen, this debate is settled: the God of Destruction is still on top.**
