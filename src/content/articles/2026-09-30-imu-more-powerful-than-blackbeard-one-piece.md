*Summary:*
- *Imu sits atop the World Government as its hidden ruler, unknown to the entire world.*
- *Blackbeard holds two Devil Fruits, including the stolen Gura Gura no Mi.*
- *Imu's recent displays of power, including Domi Reversi, suggest abilities far beyond any Yonko.*
- *We believe Imu is definitively the more powerful of the two.*

The debate over who sits at the very top of *One Piece'* power hierarchy has raged for years, and for most of that time the answer was obvious: whoever holds the title of strongest, *Blackbeard* included. But the Elbaph arc has changed the conversation completely. **We're saying it outright: Imu is more powerful than Blackbeard, and it's not particularly close.**

*Blackbeard* is a monster, make no mistake. He's the only person in history to wield **two Devil Fruits simultaneously**, the *Yami Yami no Mi* and the stolen *Gura Gura no Mi* that once belonged to *Whitebeard*. He engineered the death of a Yonko, captured *Ace*, and clawed his way from a nameless crewmate to Emperor of the Sea. That resume demands respect.

But respect isn't the same as supremacy. Here's why *Imu* holds the crown.

---

## Imu Commands Power No One Else Even Understands

Every other top-tier fighter in *One Piece*, *Blackbeard* included, operates within known systems: Haki, Devil Fruits, or both. *Imu* breaks those systems entirely. The ability to erase islands from existence, as *Lulusia* learned the hard way, isn't a Devil Fruit power anyone has been able to categorise. **Imu wields god-tier destructive capability without ever leaving the Empty Throne.**

*Blackbeard* has to physically travel to a battlefield, take punches, and grind out wins. *Imu* ends nations remotely. That's not a gap in power, that's a gap in category.

---

## Domi Reversi Shows Imu Doesn't Even Need Raw Destruction

The recent Elbaph arc gave us something even more chilling than island-erasing lasers. *Imu's* use of **Domi Reversi**, a diabolical technique that turns others into willing puppets through a covenant, demonstrates that raw force is only one string to *Imu's* bow. **The ability to corrupt and command the giants, one of the strongest warrior races in the world, means Imu can convert enemies into weapons before a single punch is thrown.**

*Blackbeard's* darkness can nullify a Devil Fruit user's powers, which is genuinely terrifying. But *Imu* doesn't need to touch you to defeat you. A commander who turns your own allies against you beats a brawler who has to close the distance every single time.

> 🤯 Imu doesn't overpower opponents, Imu rewrites them. Domi Reversi is conquest without combat.

---

## Eight Hundred Years of Unchallenged Rule

Here's the argument that settles it. *Blackbeard* has spent his entire life scheming, hiding, and waiting because he cannot take what he wants by force alone. He needed *Marshall D. Teach's* patient decades-long con, the *Shichibukai* system, and *Shiryu's* betrayal to reach the top of the food chain.

*Imu* has ruled the entire world from the shadows for **eight centuries**. The Five Elders, the strongest known Celestial Dragons, kneel before this figure without hesitation. The World Government's entire apparatus, including the Marines and Cipher Pol, exists as *Imu's* enforcement arm.

> 🙌 When the Gorosei, beings who themselves command impossible power, bow their heads to someone, that someone is the apex.

A person who needs an army to hold power is strong. A person whose power holds an entire world in silence for 800 years is something else entirely.

---

## The Verdict

*Blackbeard* is the final villain *Luffy* will likely face in a straight fistfight, and he'll probably push *Luffy* further than anyone. But being *Luffy's* destined opponent doesn't make you the strongest character in the story, it makes you the strongest *measurable* one.

*Imu* exists above the measurement. Island-erasure, puppet covenants, eight centuries of uncontested dominion over every Yonko, Marine, and Revolutionary who ever lived. **Imu is more powerful than Blackbeard, and when the story finally pulls back the curtain on the Empty Throne's full capabilities, we expect this debate to look quaint.**

That's our position, and we're not moving off it.
