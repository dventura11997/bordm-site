The first half of the new PV takes a nostalgic look back at the events of *Thousand-Year Blood War*, tracing the long road that has led *Ichigo Kurosaki* and *Yhwach* toward their final confrontation. ## The Final Two Episodes

The anime returned with its fourth and final cour, subtitled *The Calamity*, on **July 25**, with the final two episodes delayed to improve quality. The cour is set to run for **10 episodes** in total.

> 🤯 **Episode 49 airs on October 19, followed by the series finale, Episode 50, on October 26.**

According to the preview for Episode 49, *Yhwach* appears simultaneously in the Seireitei, the World of the Living, and Hueco Mundo, declaring his intention to reduce the world to nothingness. *Ichigo*, risen from the depths of despair, and his allies among the Soul Reapers and Arrancars have not given up on this world, and the final, bloody battle across all three realms begins.

---

## Special Screening Event

One day before the finale airs, a **special marathon screening and talk event will be held on October 25 at United Cinemas Aqua City Odaiba** in Japan. Scheduled guests include *Masakazu Morita*, the voice of *Ichigo Kurosaki*, *Noriaki Sugiyama*, the voice of *Uryu Ishida*, original creator *Tite Kubo*, director *Hikaru Murata*, and animation producer *Sadahiko Tominaga*.

> 📝 *Tite Kubo* has confirmed The Calamity will include even more original material than previous parts, expanding the manga's final battles.

---

## What It Means for Fans

After more than two decades since the manga debuted in *Weekly Shonen Jump*, with over **130 million copies in circulation worldwide**, the *Bleach* anime is finally about to reach its proper conclusion. The original anime famously ended before adapting the final arc back in 2012, so these last two episodes carry the weight of everything fans waited a decade for. Come October 26, the war ends.
