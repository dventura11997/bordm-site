<h2>Miroku:</h2>

  <img alt="Miroku" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/650e88099aad1a83c37df57d/65713fd959a56f76b574410a_Miroku_FA.webp" loading="lazy">

<p>Even in the enigmatic Feudal Era Japan, Inuyasha's <em>Miroku</em> stands out as a character with a penchant for mischief.</p>
<p>Trained as a monk, <em>Miroku</em> was introduced as a sharp-jawed companion, but his charisma took an unexpected turn when audiences discovered his struggle to keep his hands to himself. Although besotted with women, particularly <em>Sango, Miroku's</em> amorous tendencies often lead to comical situations, <strong>leaving anime fans amused at the countless times he's been slapped for his wandering hands.</strong></p>

<p>In the world of anime, perversion isn't exclusive to humans; even stuffed animals and intangible souls can exhibit subversive qualities. A case in point is <em>Kon</em> from <a href="/trending-topics/bleach">Bleach</a>.</p>
<p>Despite his adorable teddy bear appearance, <em>Kon</em> serves as the vessel for a <strong>rampant sexual deviant</strong>. While <em>Ichigo</em> keeps <em>Kon</em> around to house his substitute soul, the shinigami can't help but get frustrated as <em>Kon's</em> antics involve leering at breasts and conjuring up NSFW fantasies.</p>
