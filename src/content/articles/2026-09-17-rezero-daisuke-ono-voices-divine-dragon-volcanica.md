## Who Is the Divine Dragon Volcanica?

The official anime website describes Volcanica as follows:

> 📣 "The legendary 'Divine Dragon' said to have formed a pact with the king of Lugunica 400 years ago. One of the Three Great Heroes who saved the world."  -  Official Anime Website

Volcanica is said to possess immense power and play an important role in maintaining the balance of the world. Widely known and revered as the kingdom's guardian, **much about the Divine Dragon's nature remains shrouded in mystery.** Volcanica's character profile is now available on the official anime website.

---

## Daisuke Ono Joins the Cast

Ono is a heavyweight of the industry, well known for voicing characters like *Erwin Smith* in *Attack on Titan*, *Jotaro Joestar* in *JoJo's Bizarre Adventure*, and *Sebastian* in *Black Butler*, among many others. Landing a voice of his calibre for the Divine Dragon suggests the character will be far more than a passing cameo.

The main cast of Season 4 includes:

- *Kobayashi Yusuke* as *Subaru*
- *Rie Takahashi* as *Emilia*
- *Yumi Uchiyama* as *Puck*
- *Inori Minase* as *Rem*
- *Rie Murakawa* as *Ram*
- *Satomi Arai* as *Beatrice*
- *Kana Ueda* as *Anastasia*
- *Takuya Eguchi* as *Julius*
- *Fairouz Ai* as *Shaula*

---

## Season 4 Staff

The fourth season is directed by *Masahiro Shinohara*, based on the original work by *Tappei Nagatsuki* with original character designs by *Shinichiro Otsuka*. The season features series composition by *Masahiro Yokotani*, character design and chief animation direction by *Haruka Sagawa*, and animation production by **WHITE FOX**. *Kenichiro Suehiro* handles the music, with *KADOKAWA* on music production.

---
## What It Means for Fans

After first airing in 2016, adapting the light novel series by *Tappei Nagatsuki* and illustrated by *Shinichirou Otsuka*, *Re:ZERO* remains in fine form. The series has also announced **10 major projects for its 10th anniversary this year**, so there's plenty more on the horizon.

For now, fans can look forward to hearing Ono's take on one of the series' most legendary figures.
