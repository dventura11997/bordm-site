<blockquote>🔥 The Solo Leveling Anime is Set to Premiere on January 6, 2024</blockquote>
<p>Get ready for the much-anticipated <a href="/trending-topics/solo-leveling">Solo Leveling</a> anime, as it gears up for its global debut on Crunchyroll on <strong>January 6, 2024</strong>, according to an announcement from the official accounts. The exact release time for the first episode will be disclosed at a later date, building up the excitement for fans worldwide.</p>

<p>Accompanying this announcement is the release of a brand-new trailer that gives viewers a sneak peek into the anime's ending theme song, "request" by <em>krage</em>.</p>
<p>Taking the helm of the <em>Solo Leveling</em> anime adaptation is director <strong>Shunsuke Nakashige at A-1 Pictures</strong>, adapting the digital comic series published on <strong>Tappytoon by DUBU (REDICE Studio), Chugong, and h-goon</strong>. The screenplay will be crafted by <strong>Noboru Kimura</strong>, while <strong>Tomoko Sudo and Hirotaka Tokuda</strong> will be responsible for the anime character and monster designs, respectively.</p>
<p>The opening theme, "LeveL," will be performed by <em>Hiroyuki Sawano</em> and <em>TOMORROW X TOGETHER</em>, promising a captivating musical experience for fans.</p>

<p><em>Crunchyroll</em> will exclusively <strong>premiere the anime for its members in North America, Central America, South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East, and CIS</strong>. As fans await the anime, Yen Press will release the eighth volume of the Solo Leveling comic on <strong>January 23, 2024</strong>. Describing the series, <em>Yen Press</em> highlights <em>Jinwoo Sung</em>, the <strong>Weakest Hunter of All Mankind</strong>, and his struggles as an E-rank hunter. Forced by circumstances, <em>Jinwoo</em> risks his life on raids to support his family. However, an unexpected encounter with an overpowering being throws him into a perilous situation, challenging his survival skills and wits.</p>
