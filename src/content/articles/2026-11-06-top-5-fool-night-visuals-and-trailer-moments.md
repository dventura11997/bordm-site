We've watched the first *Fool Night* promotional video more times than we'd care to admit, and honestly? We're not sorry. *Kasumi Yasuda's* sci-fi drama manga is finally getting its anime, debuting worldwide exclusively on Netflix, and the promo material has us properly excited. So let's rank the visuals and trailer moments that stood out to us most.

## 5. The New Cast Portraits

The additional cast reveal gave us fresh faces to attach to the names: *Sumi Matsuno* voiced by *Shion Wakayama*, *Ken Hikasa* by *Hiroshi Tsuchida*, *Nishi* by *Nobuhiko Okamoto*, *Mei Okumura* by *Sayaka Senbongi* and *Ivy* by *Yumiko Kobayashi*. Seeing each character rendered in the anime's art style for the first time made the whole thing feel real. **That's a stacked lineup**, and the portraits showed them off beautifully.

---

## 4. The Colour Palette

There's a specific washed-out, nocturnal tone running through the promo that perfectly captures the manga's melancholy. Cool blues and muted greys dominate, giving even the quieter frames a sense of weight. It tells you immediately that **this is a drama first and a sci-fi second**, and we respect a trailer that knows its own mood.

---

## 3. The Theme Song Reveal

Music group *Tempalay* handling the music was announced alongside the promo, and the track layered under the footage does serious work. It swells at exactly the right moments, turning what could've been a standard montage into something genuinely emotional. **A great theme song is half the battle**, and this one sounds like a winner.

---

## 2. *Sumi's* Close-Ups

The trailer lingers on *Sumi Matsuno* in ways that suggest the anime understands she's the emotional core of the story. The framing in these shots is intimate and just a bit unsettling, exactly the vibe *Yasuda's* manga cultivates. If the series keeps this up, **we're in for some devastating character work**.

---

## 1. The Key Visual

Nothing encapsulates *Fool Night* better than the key visual released alongside the cast news. It distills the entire premise into a single frame: lonely, atmospheric and quietly gorgeous. It's the image that made us go from curious to counting down the days. **This is the one that sold us.**

---

Roll on November 26. If the anime delivers even half of what these visuals promise, *Fool Night* might just be the sleeper hit of the season.
