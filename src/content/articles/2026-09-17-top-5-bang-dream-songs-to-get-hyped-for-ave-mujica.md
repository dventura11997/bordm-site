With *Ave Mujica* looming on the horizon, we've been doing what any self-respecting *BanG Dream!* fan does: cranking the entire franchise discography until our speakers beg for mercy. Whether you're a *Poppin'Party* day-one or you fell down the rabbit hole via *MyGO!!!!!*, there's never been a better time to get reacquainted with the girls' band party.

*So here are the five songs we reckon best set the mood.*

## 5. Te o Tsunagou ka (Poppin'Party)

The song that started it all for so many of us. **"Te o Tsunagou ka"** is pure early *BanG Dream!* energy: scrappy, sincere and sung with the kind of imperfect conviction that made *Poppin'Party* feel like a real garage band. It's a reminder that this whole universe was built on the simple joy of picking up an instrument with your mates.

---

## 4. That Is Bana-diction! (Poppin'Party)

If you want pure hype with zero melancholy, this is the one. **Kasumi's** boundless enthusiasm absolutely barrels through the track, and the whole band sounds tighter than ever. We dare you to listen to that chorus without air-drumming. Consider it a palate cleanser before the gothic theatricality of *Ave Mujica* swallows us whole.

---

## 3. Hatsunetsu Muffler (Afterglow)

*Afterglow* don't get enough credit for being the emotional spine of the franchise, and **"Hatsunetsu Muffler"** is proof. It's a gritty, warm-blooded rock track about childhood friendship that hits harder the older you get. *Ran's* voice carries genuine grit, and it's the perfect bridge between the franchise's brighter roots and the heavier tone *MyGO* brought in.

## 2. Filament (MyGO!!!!!)

Here's where things get dark. **"Filament"** captures everything that made *MyGO!!!!!* such a gut-punch: fractured relationships, unspoken resentment and girls playing music because it's the only honest language they have left. The instrumental builds like an anxiety attack, and *Tomori's* vocals sound genuinely haunted. If *Ave Mujica* continues in this direction, this is the tone-setting track.

## 1. Symbol I : ? (Ave Mujica)

Of course the number one song to get hyped for *Ave Mujica* is an *Ave Mujica* song. **"Symbol I : ?"** announced the band with full theatrical menace: gothic dress, masked performers and a sound closer to symphonic metal than anything the franchise had attempted. It's menacing, elegant and completely unlike anything *Poppin'Party* ever dreamed of. **Every time we hear that opening, we get chills.**

---

From garage-band optimism to masked gothic spectacle, the *BanG Dream!* discography has come a long way, and *Ave Mujica* looks ready to drag it somewhere darker and grander. Queue up these five, turn the volume up, and get ready. The masquerade is about to begin.
