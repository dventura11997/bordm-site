The recent AniPlex Online Fest event spilled the beans, treating fans to a cascade of details and an enticing trailer.</p>

<h2>Blue Exorcist Season 3 Release Date: Unveiling the Shimane Illuminati Saga</h2>
<p>The much-anticipated <em>Blue Exorcist Season 3</em>, aka the <em>Shimane Illuminati Saga</em>, <strong>is set to grace our screens in January 2024</strong>. While the exact date is yet to be confirmed, fans can mark their calendars for an early 2024 release. The announcement, accompanied by a teaser trailer and a captivating key visual, unfolded at the <em>AniPlex Summer Fest</em> event. <em>AniPlex</em>, the custodians of the franchise's animated legacy, ensures that the series is in capable hands.</p>
<p>As for the duration of the new season, details remain elusive, leaving fans to speculate on whether it will match the <strong>25-episode format of the initial season.</strong></p>

<p>Blue Exorcist Season 3 will maintain continuity with its voice cast, featuring the familiar voices that have brought the characters to life throughout the series. While the confirmation is pending, the expected cast includes renowned talents such as <strong>Nobuhiko Okamoto, Jun Fukuyama, Kana Hanazawa, Kazuya Nakai, Kōji Yusa, Hiroshi Kamiya, and Keiji Fujiwara.</strong></p>
<p>The <em>Shimane Illuminati Saga</em> is undergoing animation at <em>Studio VOLN</em>, under the direction of <em>Daisuke Yoshida</em>. Notably, the shift from <em>A-1 Pictures</em>, which handled the previous seasons, raises questions about the change in studios.</p>
<hr>
<h2>Blue Exorcist Season 3 Plot: Unraveling the Shimane Illuminati Saga</h2>
<p><em>Blue Exorcist Season 3</em> will draw inspiration from volumes 10-15 (chapters 50-64) of the original manga, encompassing the <em>Shimane Illuminati arc</em>. Spoiler-free, the arc is celebrated for its intricate plot twists, unveiling a mole within the <em>True Cross</em> students aligned with the sinister organization.</p>
<p>While the arc heavily spotlights <strong>Rin and Yukio's relationship</strong>, the anime's divergence from certain manga elements leaves room for unpredictability. Fans can brace themselves for the unexpected, considering the liberties taken by the anime in altering Yukio's storyline.</p>
