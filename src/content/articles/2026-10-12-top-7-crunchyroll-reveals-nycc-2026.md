Another New York Comic Con has come and gone, and honestly, Crunchyroll may as well have set off fireworks on stage. Between game adaptations, a WEBTOON goldmine and a genuinely stacked creative team, there was a lot to chew on. So we've ranked the seven reveals that mattered, counting down to the one we still can't stop thinking about.

## 7. Sekiro's World Premiere Clip

Kicking things off, attendees got a world premiere sneak peek at *Sekiro: No Defeat*, and while a single clip is a single clip, the buzz coming out of the room was unmistakable. **Fully hand-drawn** animation for a *Sekiro* adaptation is the kind of sentence that makes us sit up and pay attention.

---

## 6. The Remarried Empress Is Getting An Anime

Buried inside the bigger announcement was the detail that made romance fans gasp: ***The Remarried Empress*** is among the titles lined up for adaptation. It's one of the most beloved WEBTOON dramas out there, and seeing it graduate to anime is a genuinely big deal for the format.

---

## 5. Teenage Mercenary and The Boxer Join The Slate

Not to be outdone, ***Teenage Mercenary*** and ***The Boxer*** were also confirmed as part of the adaptation slate. *The Boxer* in particular has some of the most kinetic fight choreography in webcomic form, and we are quietly obsessed with what that looks like animated.

---

## 4. Crunchyroll's Streaming Exclusivity Lock-In

Quietly one of the most strategically important reveals: **Crunchyroll will exclusively stream the resulting anime in select territories.** That's a lot of content locked behind one roof, and it signals just how seriously Crunchyroll is treating this co-production era.

---

## 3. The Ghost of Tsushima Legends First Look

We got our **first look at the *Ghost of Tsushima Legends* anime**, currently in production by HAYATE in collaboration with PlayStation Productions. The work-in-progress trailer gave fans an early taste of the adaptation based on the game's beloved multiplayer mode. Arriving in **2028**, it's a long wait, but the pedigree is undeniable.

## 2. Ghost of Tsushima's All-Star Creative Team

Here's where things get silly. ***Ghost of Tsushima Legends*** is led by director *Takanobu Mizuno*, with *Gen Urobuchi* handling series composition, **2026 Emmy Award winner *Takashi Okazaki*** as character designer, *Keiji Inai* composing and *Yoshikazu Iwanami* on sound. Add newly announced voice cast members *Mayumi Saco* and *Shinya Fukumatsu*, and this is one of the most impressive teams ever assembled for a game adaptation.

## 1. The WEBTOON Co-Production Deal

And the number one spot goes to the announcement that changes everything: **Crunchyroll, HAYATE and LINE Digital Frontier are partnering to adapt around 15 WEBTOON series into anime.** This isn't a one-off licence grab, it's a full framework where all three companies collaborate **from the development stage**, with HAYATE leading production.

> 🤯 Around 15 WEBTOON adaptations, developed jointly from the ground up, streaming exclusively on Crunchyroll.

If this deal delivers even half of what it promises, the anime landscape of the late 2020s is going to look dramatically different. WEBTOON readers have waited years for this level of commitment, and honestly? It's about time.
