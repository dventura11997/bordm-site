<p>In chapter #243 of <a href="/trending-topics/jujutsu-kaisen">Jujutsu Kaisen</a>, the elusive mastermind <em>Kenjaku</em> makes a critical error. His intense focus on the battle against <em>Takaba</em> leads to a fatal lapse in his defenses, allowing <em>Yuta</em> to swiftly incapacitate him with a single strike.</p>

  <img alt="Chapter #243, Jujutsu Kaisen" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/650e88099aad1a83c37df57d/656e977f0d4426e327671f9a_jujutsu-kaisen_yutas-sneak-attack-on-kenjaku.webp" loading="lazy">

<p>While it's true that this strategic move was made possible by <em>Takaba's</em> cursed technique, the significance lies in <em>Yuta</em> delivering the decisive blow to <em>Kenjaku</em>. Perhaps, with this development, the original protagonist of <em>Jujutsu Kaisen</em> is poised to reclaim his central role, and the timing couldn't be more crucial. <a href="/articles/fans-in-shock-as-writer-of-jujutsu-kaisen-kills-the-beloved-gojo">Following Gojo's demise</a>, sorcerers are grappling with the loss of their primary asset, and <strong>Yuta emerges as the most promising candidate to fill the void left by his mentor.</strong></p>
<blockquote><em>🤯 Yuta's</em> resurgence underscores his newfound status as the most powerful sorcerer in Jujutsu Kaisen.</blockquote>
<p>Contrary to popular belief, the series didn't commence with <em>Yuji's</em> encounter with <em>Sukuna's</em> finger; it initially revolved around <a href="/articles/unraveling-the-mystery-yuta-okkotsus-absence-in-jujutsu-kaisen-0"><em>Yuta Okkotsu</em> in the limited series <em>"Cursed Child."</em></a> Despite <em>Yuta's</em> promising trajectory, the narrative shifted to new characters, relegating him to the background. However, the recent events highlight that circumstances demand <em>Yuta</em> to <strong>step into a more prominent role.</strong></p>

  <img alt="Yuta Okkotsu" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/650e88099aad1a83c37df57d/656e97b2a832bfb83dca7302_jujutsu-kaisen_yutas-black-flash.webp" loading="lazy">

<p>His defeat of <em>Kenjaku</em> in Chapter #243 showcases <em>Yuta's</em> exceptional abilities. Despite the brevity of the encounter, it speaks volumes about his prowess. Facing the formidable <em>"cursed technique reversal: anti-gravity,"</em> <em>Yuta</em> demonstrated both speed and strength, evading its full effects and <strong>decapitating Kenjaku with a single swing.</strong></p>
<p>The confrontation also underscores <em>Yuta's</em> profound understanding of sorcery mechanics. Targeting the enemy's head, he recognized that, given <em>Kenjaku's</em> mastery of reverse cursed technique, <strong>swift decapitation was the only way to ensure an instantaneous kill</strong>. Whether <em>Gege Akutami</em> will fully return to <em>Yuta's</em> initial story remains to be seen, <strong>but his swift victory over <em>Kenjaku</em> unequivocally establishes Yuta as the new powerhouse in the world of Jujutsu Kaisen.</strong></p>
