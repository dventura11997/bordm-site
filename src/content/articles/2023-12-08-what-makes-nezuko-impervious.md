<p>At the heart of the story is <em>Tanjiro Kamado</em>, the protagonist on a mission to eradicate demons from the world. Central to the narrative is his younger sister, <em>Nezuko Kamado</em>, who was transformed into a demon by <a href="/articles/how-muzan-the-first-demon-in-demon-slayer-accidentally-embraced-darkness">Muzan Kibutsuji.</a></p>
<p>Demons typically wither away in daylight, succumbing to the sun's destructive rays. Yet, <em>Nezuko</em> defies this norm, earning her the moniker;</p>
<blockquote>🤯 "The Chosen Demon."</blockquote>
<p>While the exact reason for her immunity remains elusive, various theories attempt to unravel this mystery. Despite the <em>Kamado</em> family's lack of a direct connection to <em>Yoriichi Tsukiguni,</em> the original <a href="/trending-topics/demon-slayer">Demon Slayer</a> and creator of the <strong>Sun Breathing technique</strong>, they incorporate Sun Breathing in an annual ritual.</p>
<p>This connection could explain why <em>Nezuko's</em> Blood Demon Art manifests as fire, <strong>underscoring her ties to the Sun Breathing lineage.</strong></p>

<p>Remarkably, <em>Nezuko</em>, despite her demonic nature, <strong>abstains from consuming human flesh,</strong> distinguishing her as the only thriving demon without such a diet.</p>
<p>In a pivotal moment during the battle with <em>Upper Rank 4 Demon Hantengu in Chapter 126 of Demon Slayer, Nezuko</em> finds herself exposed to direct sunlight. While this scenario would typically prove fatal, <strong>her resistance to sunlight becomes evident.</strong></p>
<p>It's worth noting that <em>Nezuko</em> <strong>wasn't always immune to sunlight;</strong> she initially suffered severe burns. <strong>Ultimately, her lineage's connection to Sun Breathing and her independence from human sustenance likely contribute to her newfound immunity.</strong></p>
<p><em>Muzan's</em> quest for the <a href="/articles/how-muzan-the-first-demon-in-demon-slayer-accidentally-embraced-darkness">Blue Spider Lilly</a>, a key ingredient in the doctor's life-saving medicine, highlights his pursuit of the perfect human vessel. This vessel would ideally be immune to sunlight, allowing <em>Muzan</em> to consume them and achieve perfection.</p>

<p><em>Nezuko</em> stands out as <strong>the only demon outside Muzan Kibutsuji's control,</strong> joining <em>Tamayo</em> in this unique position. The <em>Upper Moon Demons</em> and other minions are expressly forbidden from divulging information about <em>Muzan</em> to their adversaries.</p>
<p><em>Muzan</em> possesses unparalleled knowledge about the demons he creates, monitoring their observations, experiences, and thoughts. This advantage significantly elevates <em>Nezuko's</em> strength above that of her demonic counterparts.</p>
