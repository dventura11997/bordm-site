Hold onto your star charts, because two absolute titans of Japanese animation are joining forces on the *Yamato* franchise. A website opened on Tuesday to announce that **Hideaki Anno and Yutaka Izubuchi are directing the feature-length anime film *Cosmoship Yamato √2199*** at **Production I.G and Studio Khara.**

For the uninitiated, *Anno* is the mastermind behind *Neon Genesis Evangelion* and the architect of Studio Khara itself, while *Izubuchi* made his name as the director of *RahXephon* and a legendary designer across decades of the franchise. The film's Japanese title uses the same "Uchū Senkan" prefix that has previously been translated as both "Space Battleship" and "Space Cruiser," so expect English-language outlets to settle on their own renderings before an official one lands.

---

## The Staff Lineup

Beyond the co-directors, the announcement came loaded with credits that should have longtime fans buzzing. **Production I.G founder Mitsuhisa Ishikawa is also credited with planning alongside Anno.** *Anno* himself is pulling double duty as **executive producer**, while the production is being handled through **Shin Office Academy**, with the animation coming out of **Production I.G and Khara.**

The original work is credited to **Yoshinobu Nishizaki**, the original co-creator of *Uchuu Senkan Yamato*, anchoring the new film to the franchise's roots even as it charts fresh territory.

---

## A Different Path From the Remakes

Perhaps the most intriguing detail to come out of the announcement is *Anno's* own framing of the project. **Anno stated that *Uchuu Senkan Yamato √2199* is being developed separately and will take a different path from the remakes.**

> 🤯 This is not another entry in the *2199* remake continuity, it's a standalone vision with Anno at the helm as both director and executive producer.

That's a significant distinction. The *Space Battleship Yamato* franchise has been steadily rebuilt over the past decade-plus beginning with *Uchuu Senkan Yamato 2199*, which rebooted the original 1974 series. An *Anno*-led film that deliberately steps away from that remake lineage suggests something far more personal, and given his history with *Evangelion* and his *Shin* treatments of beloved properties, that prospect is genuinely exciting.

---

## Some Franchise Context

The original *Uchuu Senkan Yamato* television anime aired in **26 episodes from October 1974 to March 1975**, with *Leiji Matsumoto's* original manga running alongside it. Sequels and remakes followed across the decades, including *Michio Murakawa's* remake manga which began serialising in March 2012. English-speaking fans have been able to enjoy both the original manga, released by Seven Seas Entertainment as an omnibus, and the remake manga via Dark Horse Comics.

A teaser visual accompanied the announcement, though **no premiere date has been revealed at this stage.**

---

## What It Means for Fans

An *Anno* and *Izubuchi* collaboration on *Yamato* is the kind of pairing that once lived purely in fans' wildest forum fantasies. Both creators have deep, storied connections to the franchise and to mecha and sci-fi anime more broadly, and with **Production I.G and Khara sharing production duties**, the talent on this one is stacked from top to bottom.

We'll be keeping a close eye on the official site for a release window and further staff announcements. In the meantime, the wait begins.
