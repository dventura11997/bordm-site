Naruto fans, start your engines, because the Hidden Leaf Village is about to get a whole lot bigger. The "NARUTO: What's Next?" panel at New York Comic Con announced on Saturday that **Masashi Kishimoto is launching a new manga in his Naruto franchise**, with a teaser video pointing towards the title *Naruto Shadow Rising*.

## A New Era From Kishimoto Himself

This is genuinely massive news. We're not talking about a spin-off or a side project, this is a **brand new manga from the creator of Naruto**, the series that defined a generation of shonen fans. *Kishimoto* originally serialised *Naruto* in *Weekly Shounen Jump* from September 1999 to November 2014, with the 72nd and final volume published in February 2015. The manga boasts a colossal **250 million copies in circulation** worldwide.

Since then, the franchise has continued through *Boruto: Naruto Next Generations*, which launched in May 2016 with story by *Ukyou Kodachi* and art by *Mikio Ikemoto*, before *Kishimoto* took over writing duties in November 2020. Its sequel, *Boruto: Two Blue Vortex*, began in August 2023 with *Kishimoto* and *Ikemoto* at the helm.

> 🤯 The teaser video points to the new manga being titled *Naruto Shadow Rising*.

## Part of the Bigger Picture

The new manga is just one piece of a much larger puzzle. According to the panel, the project is part of **Project Rasengan**, a three-year timeline of upcoming releases within the Naruto franchise. Already announced under the banner are a crossover anime series, a mobile game and a card game.

The crossover anime, revealed as *NARUTO×NINKŪ*, teams the franchise up with *Koji Kiriyama's* classic ninja manga *Ninku*, which the panel noted **actually inspired Kishimoto to create Naruto in the first place**. Talk about a full-circle moment.

---

## When Do We Learn More?

Patience will be required, sadly. **More details on the new manga will be revealed at Jump Festa '27, which takes place on December 19-20.**

> 📝 Note: While the teaser video points towards the title *Naruto Shadow Rising*, treat it as a tease for now until Shueisha confirms it properly.

With a new manga from Kishimoto, a crossover anime and a stacked slate of projects under Project Rasengan, the next three years are shaping up as a golden era for Naruto fans. Believe it!
