## A Franchise Built on Interstitial Stories

Since the original *Bunny Girl Senpai* TV anime, the franchise has adapted *Hajime Kamoshida's* light novels in order: the *Daughter* arc, the *Hakodate* arc, and the twin film arcs, *Rascal Does Not Dream of a Dreaming Girl* and *Rascal Does Not Dream of a Sister Venturing Out*. Each adaptation has been careful to preserve the novel continuity, which means anything written by *Kamoshida* himself counts as canon.

Bonus text stories have precedent here. The series has previously used short-form prose to develop side characters and set up future arcs, and these stories tend to slot between major plot beats rather than retcon anything. The *Dear Friend* bonus story follows that same pattern.

## What the Bonus Story Actually Is

The text story was announced as a **gift item handed to theatre-goers attending *Rascal Does Not Dream of a Dear Friend***, a standard Japanese theatrical practice known as a moviegoer bonus (*boki*). These novella-style handouts are typically printed in booklet form and given out during a specific screening window.

Because the story comes directly from the production side of the franchise rather than a licensed anthology, it occupies the same canonical tier as the light novels themselves.

> 🤯 Bonus stories like this one aren't promotional fluff, they're primary franchise material distributed through cinemas instead of bookstores.

The film also made headlines for streaming its **first four minutes in advance**, a move that gave audiences an early taste of the story while the bonus novella quietly carried the connective tissue.

## Where It Fits in the Timeline

*Dear Friend* adapts a later arc of the novel series, which means the bonus story most naturally sits alongside the film's events, either expanding on a character's interiority during the arc or bridging the gap toward the next one. Given the franchise's structure, there are three possibilities for any such text:

- **A prelude** that sets up the emotional state of characters heading into the film
- **A side story** running parallel to the film's main plot, focusing on a supporting cast member
- **An epilogue** that plants seeds for the following arc

All three are well-established moves for this franchise, and none of them would contradict the film itself.

## What It Means Going Forward

For fans watching the films internationally, the practical takeaway is simple: **the bonus story is part of the canon, and it may never be officially translated.** The *Rascal Does Not Dream* franchise has a history of its ancillary material remaining Japan-only, which means dedicated fans often rely on the community to piece together what these texts contain.

If the story introduces new details about a character's Adolescence Syndrome or teases an unresolved thread, it could colour how audiences interpret the next film. Given how carefully *Kamoshida* has layered his arcs, from *Kaede's* recovery to *Mai's* career, dismissing a bonus novella as merch would be a mistake.

The franchise has always rewarded close readers, and a text story handed out in cinemas is exactly the kind of detail that turns out to matter two films later.
